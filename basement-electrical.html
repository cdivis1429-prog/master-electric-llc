What's included

  • Lighting and outlet layout planned with you
  • Electrical permit pulled and inspections scheduled
  • Recessed LED lighting, dimmers and switches
  • Bedroom, bathroom, bar, office and media room circuits
  • AFCI/GFCI protection and smoke/CO detectors to current code
  • Subpanels or panel upgrades if your panel is full
  • Low-voltage prep for TV, data and speakers
  • Exhaust fans, heated floors and dedicated appliance circuits

How a basement electrical job works

We start with a walkthrough to plan where lighting, outlets, switches and any dedicated circuits go. After the permit is issued, we rough-in the wiring once framing is up and get the rough electrical inspection passed so drywall can start. After drywall and paint, we come back to install fixtures, devices and trim, then schedule the final inspection.

Working with basement companies and builders

If you're a basement finisher, remodeler or builder, we can be your electrical sub. We show up when scheduled, keep inspections moving and price jobs clearly so you can bid with confidence.

Common questions

Do I need a permit to finish my basement electrical?

Yes. In Aurora, Denver and the surrounding cities, new basement wiring requires an electrical permit and inspections. We pull the permit and schedule the inspections for you.

How long does basement electrical take?

Rough-in for a typical basement takes a few days, then trim-out takes a day or two after drywall. Inspection scheduling with the city is usually the biggest variable.

Will my panel have room for a finished basement?

Often yes, but not always. We check your panel's capacity and open spaces during the walkthrough and will tell you up front if you need a subpanel or upgrade.

Do you work with my contractor or basement company?

Yes. We regularly work as the electrical subcontractor for basement finishers and remodelers, and we also work directly for homeowners.
