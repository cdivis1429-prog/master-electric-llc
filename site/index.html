Basement finishes

Finishing your basement? We do the electrical start to finish.

Basement finishes are the core of our work. We plan the lighting and outlet layout with you, pull the permit, rough-in for framing inspection, and come back to trim out once drywall is done.

  • Recessed LED lighting and dimmers
  • Bedroom, bathroom, bar and media room circuits
  • Code-required smoke/CO detectors and AFCI protection
  • Subpanels and panel upgrades when you're out of space
  • We work with homeowners, basement companies and remodelers
