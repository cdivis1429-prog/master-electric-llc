About Master Electric

A locally owned electrical contractor in Aurora, Colorado, built on doing the work right, showing up when we say we will and pricing jobs fairly.

Local, licensed and hands-on

Master Electric, LLC is a licensed Colorado electrical contractor (#EC.0103012) serving the Denver Metro area. With more than 20 years in the electrical trade, we handle residential work, including basement finishes, remodels, panel upgrades and repairs, along with commercial tenant improvements and service.

When you call, you talk to the owner. We give clear written estimates, pull the permits, and stay with the job until it passes inspection.

Faith-driven. Family-owned.

Master Electric is a family-owned company built on faith in Jesus Christ. For us, that isn't about religion. It's about following Him in how we work: being honest about what a job needs and what it will cost, keeping our word, and treating every home and jobsite with respect.

We try to do every job as if we were doing it for the Lord, not just for a paycheck. That's where "Done right. No callbacks." comes from.
