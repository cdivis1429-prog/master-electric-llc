Common questions

How long does a panel replacement take?

Most residential panel swaps are completed in one day. Service upgrades that need utility coordination can take longer to schedule.

Can I install an EV charger on my current panel?

Sometimes. We check your panel capacity and load. If there's room we install the charger on its own circuit; if not, we'll explain your options, including load management or an upgrade.

Is a panel upgrade permitted?

Yes. Panel and service work requires a permit and inspection, which we handle.
