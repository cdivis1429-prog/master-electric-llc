Common questions

Do kitchen remodels need a permit?

Most kitchen and bathroom remodels that add or change circuits require an electrical permit. We handle permits and inspections.

Can you update the wiring in an older home during a remodel?

Yes. Remodels are the best time to replace outdated wiring, add grounding and GFCI protection, and add circuits for modern appliances.

Do you work with general contractors?

Yes. We work with remodelers and general contractors as their electrical sub, and directly with homeowners.
